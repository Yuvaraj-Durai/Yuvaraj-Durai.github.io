Introduction

A researcher's foundation, an entrepreneur's outlook

I completed my B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering at Rajalakshmi Institute of Technology, Anna University, Chennai (2021–2025). I'm now in the second semester of my first year of a PhD in Electrical & Electronics Engineering at BITS Pilani, K. K. Birla Goa Campus, conducting my research there under Dr. Shivin Srivastava.

My research sits at the intersection of intelligent systems and hardware — Machine Learning, Deep Learning and AI, alongside semiconductor devices, IoT, embedded systems and RTOS. Alongside this, I've had an interest in business since childhood, and I'm using this stage of my research career to deliberately build toward starting a company in the future, rather than treating the two paths as separate.

Outside the lab, I've picked up practical, hands-on hardware skills — PCB design and soldering, from prototyping through assembly — through internships, coursework and independent projects. I've also worked a range of part-time roles during my undergraduate years, which taught me discipline and how to work directly with people.
